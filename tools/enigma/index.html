    Models the three-rotor Wehrmacht Enigma I with rotors I–V, reflectors B and C, ring settings and plugboard, including the middle rotor's double step. The defaults (I-II-III, UKW-B, rings AAA, start AAA) turn HELLOWORLD into ILBDA AMTAZ, matching the historical reference.
