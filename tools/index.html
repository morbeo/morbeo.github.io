Tools

Small browser-based utilities. Everything runs locally in your browser.

PS5 Controller Debugger /tools/pad/

Test a DualSense: buttons, sticks, triggers, circularity, deadzones, rumble, touchpad, mic and speaker, plus a fighting-game move detector.
Enigma I Simulator /tools/enigma/

Encode and decode with a three-rotor Enigma I, then step through each keypress: plugboard, rotors, double step and reflector, with a signal path diagram.
Guitar Theory Lab /tools/guitar/

Scales, modes, intervals and an arpeggiator on a fretboard for guitar, bass and ukulele (overlaid or stacked), plus a tuner with presets, custom tunings and microphone pitch detection.
